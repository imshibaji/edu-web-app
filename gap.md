A "gap" in an online teaching portal refers to missing features, communication disconnects, or learning deficiencies where the digital system fails to match the effectiveness of a real-world classroom. It highlights shortcomings in technology, student engagement, or content delivery. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]  
Common Types of Gaps 

• Interaction Gaps: A lack of real-time, face-to-face body language and spontaneous discussions. 
• Technological Gaps: Unequal access to fast internet or good devices, known as the digital divide. 
• Feedback Gaps: Delays in how fast teachers can review work and reply to student questions. 
• Skill Gaps: Students missing foundational practice needed to understand new online lessons. [2, 4, 6, 7, 8]  

How Portals Try to Fix Gaps 

• Adding live video rooms and instant chat features. 
• Using built-in analytics to spot student score drops early. 
• Blending self-paced modules with live instructor sessions. [5, 9, 10]
